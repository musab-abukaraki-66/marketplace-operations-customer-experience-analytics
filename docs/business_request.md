# Simulated stakeholder request

> **Portfolio case study.** This request is fictional and written by the project author. No real company commissioned this work and no stakeholder approved its outputs.

**From:** Marketplace leadership team (simulated)
**Subject:** What is going on in our marketplace, and where should we act first?

We run an online marketplace connecting independent sellers with customers across Brazil. Growth has been good, but we hear mixed signals: some customers are unhappy with deliveries, and we don't know whether problems are concentrated in particular categories, regions or sellers.

Please help us understand how orders and sales have developed, how reliable our deliveries are, whether delivery problems show up in customer reviews, how many customers come back, and which sellers or segments deserve a closer look. We need a short list of priorities, with the evidence behind them and a clear statement of what the data cannot tell us.
